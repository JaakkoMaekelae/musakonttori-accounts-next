# Musakonttori HQ

## Vision

Musakonttori HQ becomes the internal command center for the complete Musakonttori ecosystem.

It must give management, product, support, finance and engineering one place to understand what is happening across every product.

## Products connected to HQ

- [[10 Stageflow]]
- [[11 Ticketing]]
- [[12 LiveGuide]]
- [[13 SmartLink]]
- [[14 Market]]
- [[15 SoundLaunch]]
- [[16 Mastering]]

## Future role

HQ should become the cross-product operating system for Musakonttori.

It should answer:

- Which products are growing?
- Which products generate revenue?
- Which customers use several products?
- What requires attention today?
- Where are errors happening?
- Which support cases are urgent?
- What features are customers requesting?
- What products should be developed next?
- What incidents are active?
- What is the health of every product?
